About this site

Learn Signals is a personal study site for AAE 301, Signal Analysis for Aerospace Engineering, at Purdue. It is built by Michael Greenlee to learn the material by making it interactive, in the same style as the companion site on hypersonics.

What is here

Eleven lessons covering the whole course, each with live figures, worked examples and a problem generator: Exam 1 (complex numbers, inner products and orthogonal expansions, Fourier series, the DFT/FFT), Exam 2 (the Laplace transform chapter: transforms, partial fractions and ODEs, LTI systems and transfer functions with circuits and stability) and the Final (state space, sinusoid response and Bode plots, filters, the Fourier transform and the uncertainty principle). The Exam 1 and Exam 2 checklists use the official learning objectives. There is a review page for each exam, with an objectives checklist, a formula sheet, exam-style problems and a practice-exam generator. A practice page mixes every problem type, and five calculators cover complex numbers, Fourier series, fft/ifft, partial fractions and transfer functions.

How the problem generators work

Each topic has a generator that picks random parameters, writes the question, computes the exact answer, and writes a worked solution with those numbers. Answers are parsed as complex expressions and compared numerically, with a tolerance of about 0.2%. Angles are compared modulo 2π. The test script tests/validate.js runs every generator thousands of times in Node and checks each answer against an independent calculation where one exists: numerical integration for Fourier coefficients, MATLAB-style ifft for DFT problems, and direct evaluation for identities.

Sources and conventions

The notation follows the course textbook, A. E. Frazho, Notes on Signals and Systems, the Laplace-transform lecture notes by D. Sun and J. Goppert, and the AAE 301 lectures. The Fourier series is \(f = \sum a_ke^{-ik\omega_0t}\) with \(a_k = \frac1\tau\int_0^\tau fe^{ik\omega_0t}dt\), and the DFT uses MATLAB's fft/ifft. This site is a study aid, not an official course page. The practice problems are original problems in the style of the course's exams, not reproductions of them. If anything here disagrees with your instructor or the notes, trust them, and please report the error.
