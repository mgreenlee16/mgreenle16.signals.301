About this site

Learn Signals is a personal study site for AAE 301, Signal Analysis for Aerospace Engineering, at Purdue. It is built by Michael Greenlee to learn the material by making it interactive, in the same style as the companion site on hypersonics.

What is here

Exam 1 material is complete: four lessons (complex numbers, inner products and orthogonal expansions, Fourier series, and the DFT/FFT), each with live figures, worked examples and a problem generator; an Exam 1 review page with an objectives checklist, formula sheet, exam-style problems and a practice-exam generator; a practice page; and three calculators. Lessons for Exam 2 (Laplace transforms, transfer functions, state space) and the final (frequency response, filters, the Fourier transform) will be added closer to those exams.

How the problem generators work

Each topic has a generator that picks random parameters, writes the question, computes the exact answer, and writes a worked solution with those numbers. Answers are parsed as complex expressions and compared numerically, with a tolerance of about 0.2%. Angles are compared modulo 2π. The test script tests/validate.js runs every generator thousands of times in Node and checks each answer against an independent calculation where one exists: numerical integration for Fourier coefficients, MATLAB-style ifft for DFT problems, and direct evaluation for identities.

Sources and conventions

The notation follows the course notes, A. E. Frazho, Notes on Signals and Systems, and the AAE 301 lectures. The Fourier series is \(f = \sum a_ke^{-ik\omega_0t}\) with \(a_k = \frac1\tau\int_0^\tau fe^{ik\omega_0t}dt\), and the DFT uses MATLAB's fft/ifft. This site is a study aid, not an official course page. The practice problems are original problems in the style of the course's exams, not reproductions of them. If anything here disagrees with your instructor or the notes, trust them, and please report the error.
