Lessons

The lessons are grouped by exam. Work through them in order: each one uses the one before. Lesson 1's complex arithmetic and Lesson 2's orthogonal expansions are the language of Lesson 3's Fourier series, and Lesson 4 computes Fourier series from samples. Notes

Using the site

Conventions. Everything follows the course notes (A. E. Frazho, Notes on Signals and Systems): \(f(t) = \sum a_ke^{-ik\omega_0t}\) with \(a_k = \frac1\tau\int_0^\tau f(t)e^{ik\omega_0t}dt\), the normalized inner product \((f,g) = \frac1\tau\int_0^\tau f\bar g\,dt\), and MATLAB's fft/ifft. Some lecture slides use \(e^{+jk\omega_0t}\) instead, which flips the sign in the derivative and shift rules. The lessons point this out where it matters.

Answers. Problem generators accept exact expressions such as sqrt(3)/2, -pi/4, 2-3i or e^(-pi/2), and accept any angle equal modulo 2π. Your progress is stored only in this browser. Theme and text size (top right) are remembered too.

Exam problems. The practice problems are written in the style of past exams and the course's practice test, with new numbers each time. They are not copies of real exam questions.
