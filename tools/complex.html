How to type expressions

Use i or j for \(\sqrt{-1}\), pi, e, + - * / ^, implicit multiplication (2i, 3(1+i)), |z|, and the functions sqrt exp log ln sin cos tan sinh cosh conj re im abs angle. Powers like a^b use the principal logarithm, \(a^b = e^{b\ln a}\); integer powers are computed exactly. Angles are in radians. See Lesson 1 for the methods.
