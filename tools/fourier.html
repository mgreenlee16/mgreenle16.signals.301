Notes

Coefficients are computed by Gauss–Legendre quadrature, \(a_k = \frac1\tau\int_0^\tau f(t)e^{ik\omega_0t}dt\), with the interval split at the points you list. List every jump or kink for full accuracy. Use if(condition, a, b) for piecewise signals, and t as the variable. For real \(f\): \(\alpha_k = 2\Re a_k\), \(\beta_k = 2\Im a_k\), \(\gamma_k = 2|a_k|\), \(\psi_k = \angle(\alpha_k + i\beta_k)\). If your problem uses the slides' \(e^{+jk\omega_0t}\) convention, its \(a_k\) is this table's \(a_{-k}\). See Lesson 3.
