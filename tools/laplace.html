How to enter polynomials

Use MATLAB coefficient vectors, highest power first ([1 3 2] is \(s^2 + 3s + 2\)), products with conv([1 2],[1 3]), or type a polynomial in \(s\) directly, such as (s+1)(s^2+2s+5). The numerator may have the same degree as the denominator; the extra constant is the direct term \(k\), which becomes \(k\,\delta(t)\). The method is explained in Lesson 6.
