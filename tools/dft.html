Conventions

fft(y) \(= F_\nu y\) with \(F_\nu[j,k] = e^{-2\pi ijk/\nu}\), and ifft(y) \(= F_\nu^{-1}y = \frac1\nu\overline{F_\nu}y\). When \(y\) samples a \(\tau\)-periodic signal at \(t_j = j\tau/\nu\), ifft(y) holds the Fourier coefficients \(a_k\) of \(f = \sum a_ke^{-ik\omega_0t}\) (exactly, for a trigonometric polynomial with \(\nu \ge 2K+1\)). Slot \(r\) (index \(r+1\)) holds harmonic \(r\) for \(r \le \nu/2\) and \(r - \nu\) above that. With a sampling rate, harmonic \(h\) is \(hF_s/\nu\) Hz. See Lesson 4.
